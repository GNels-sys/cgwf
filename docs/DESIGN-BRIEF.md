# CGwF design refresh brief

You are redesigning the look and feel of CGwF ("Card Games with Friends"), a private, installable web app where four friends play Commander (a Magic: The Gathering format) on a synced virtual table. The app works; your job is to make it *feel like sitting down at a card table*: rich, tactile, a little magical, with beautiful imagery and tasteful motion. Functionality must not change.

## Where it lives

- Repository: `C:\Openclaw\cgwf` (git, branch `main`). Node 24, npm workspaces: `shared/`, `server/`, `client/`.
- Read `CLAUDE.md` first (architecture, rules, conventions), then `README.md`, then `docs/` and `side-session-notes/`.
- Run locally: `npm install`, `npm run build -w shared`, then `npm run dev` (API on :8080, client on :5173 with proxy). Tests: `npm test`. Types: `npm run typecheck`. Production build: `npm run build`.
- The app is live at https://cgwf.fly.dev. Do **not** deploy; see "Process" below.

## What you may change (the design surface)

All under `client/`:

- `client/src/styles.css`: global theme tokens (`--bg`, `--bg-elev`, `--fg`, `--accent`, `--border`, `--radius`, `--mana-*` ...), base typography, light/dark variants. Build the new design system here first.
- `client/src/pages.css`, `client/src/game.css`, `client/src/stats.css`, `client/src/admin.css`: all page, table and chart styling.
- Markup and class names in `client/src/components/*.tsx`, `client/src/pages/*.tsx` (including the owner-only `client/src/pages/AdminPage.tsx` with its `client/src/admin.css`: stat tiles, two CSS column charts, a players table that collapses to cards under 840 px) and the presentational parts of `client/src/game/*.tsx` (Battlefield, CardTile, Table, Panels, Sheets, Lobby, LifeWidget, Mana, Triggers, TokenFinder, CardZoom, AgentsPanel, SettingsPanel). Restructure JSX for layout and visuals as needed, but keep every button, action and piece of information that exists today.
- `client/public/`: icons, favicon, and new imagery under `client/public/art/`. `client/index.html` (meta, theme colour, fonts) and the PWA manifest colours/icons in `client/vite.config.ts`.
- Presets for table theme / playmat / sleeve: you may restyle the existing ones and add new ones. Their keys and labels live in `shared/src/settings.ts` (lists) and `client/src/game/look.ts` (labels/CSS mapping); add new presets in both places and keep the existing keys working so saved user settings do not break.
- Motion: CSS transitions and keyframes are welcome (cards sliding between zones, tap rotation, life ticks, trigger highlights, a soft table ambience). Keep it cheap and never blocking: no animation may delay or hide an action. Everything must respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (the app already exposes `prefersReducedMotion()` in `client/src/settings.tsx`). If you believe a JavaScript animation library is needed, stop and propose it first.

## What you must not touch

- `shared/src/game/**` (reducer, view filter, protocol): the rules of the table. `server/**`: the API, database and agents. Database migrations. `Dockerfile`, `fly.toml`, `.github/`.
- Client logic files: `client/src/api.ts`, `client/src/user.tsx`, `client/src/game/model.ts`, `client/src/game/socket.ts`, `client/src/game/actions.ts`. Styling may wrap them; behaviour may not change.
- `client/src/components/Footer.tsx`: the Fan Content Policy notice and the Scryfall credit are legally required on every page. Restyle, never shorten or hide.
- `client/src/components/CardImage.tsx` behaviour: card images are shown whole at the real card ratio (63:88), never cropped, filtered, recoloured, darkened or overlaid in a way that hides the artist line or copyright. Rounded corners, shadows and a frame around the card are fine.
- `client/src/components/ManaCost.tsx` approach: mana symbols are plain coloured dots by the owner's decision. You may refine the dot styling through the `--mana-*` tokens, but do not introduce symbol artwork.
- Licensing (non-negotiable): no Wizards of the Coast logos, trademarks, mana symbols, set symbols or card art anywhere in the UI or assets. No fonts or marks that imitate the official brand. Any new imagery must be original, generated by you, or clearly licensed CC0/public domain; record the source of every asset in `docs/DESIGN.md`.

## Design direction

- Mood: a well-lit game night. Depth and texture (felt, wood, stone, velvet are existing table themes; make them gorgeous), warm accent light, cards that feel physical. Dark mode is primary; light mode must remain usable.
- Hierarchy on the table: the player's own battlefield and hand are the hero; opponents are glanceable; life, mana bank and triggers are readable at arm's length on a phone.
- Devices, in priority order: phone landscape (812x375), phone portrait (375x812), desktop. Touch targets at least 44px. Text readable at phone size; WCAG AA contrast.
- Keep the first-run promise: a new player can start with a name and avatar in ten seconds. The welcome screen should sell the game night feeling in one glance.

## Process

1. Create a branch: `git checkout -b design-refresh`. Commit in meaningful steps with clear messages.
2. Before every commit: `npm run typecheck` and `npm test` must pass, `npm run build` must succeed. There are 193 tests; they are the safety net, do not edit them to make them pass.
3. Verify visually at the three sizes above, in dark and light mode, and with reduced motion on. Include screenshots in your report.
4. Write `docs/DESIGN.md`: tokens, type scale, spacing, motion rules, asset sources.
5. Push the branch and stop. Do **not** run `fly deploy` and do not merge to `main`; the owner reviews and deploys. Never commit `.env`, anything from `C:\Openclaw\cgwf-secrets`, or files under `data/`.
6. If something in this brief conflicts with what you find in the code, stop and ask rather than guessing.
